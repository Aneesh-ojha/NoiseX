Domains

Applications

Aviation

Cockpit and ground crew communication in high-turbine-noise flight environments. Improves intelligibility during critical flight phases.

Defence

Tactical radio communication in combat environments with gunfire, explosions, and composite battlefield acoustic clutter.

Rotorcraft

Helicopter crew intercom affected by blade-passing harmonics (12–25 Hz) and turboshaft whine (2–5 kHz).

Industrial

Heavy machinery and manufacturing environments where continuous mechanical noise degrades intercom intelligibility.
